Data Controller

enimaloc <contact [at] enimaloc.fr>

Data collected

  • OAuth Twitch token — used to interact with the Twitch API (stream category, events)
  • Steam ID — used to read your game library via the Steam API
  • Error reports — when a page hits an error: your account id, the trail of your last actions (clicks, pages, requests) and a recording of the page, without what you type in fields, to reproduce the problem

Data Transfers

Your data is sent only to the Twitch and Steam APIs for the application to function, and error reports to Sentry (sentry.io), through Catapult: your browser never contacts Sentry directly. No other third-party sharing.

Retention Period

Your data is kept for the lifetime of your account and deleted when you delete it; error reports for 90 days at most.

Your Rights

You can delete your account from the Settings page. For any request, contact the administrator by email.
