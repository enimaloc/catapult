Data Controller

enimaloc <contact [at] enimaloc.fr>

Data collected

  • OAuth Twitch token — used to interact with the Twitch API (stream category, events)
  • Steam ID — used to read your game library via the Steam API
  • speedrun.com username and API key (both optional) — used to look up your records on speedrun.com; the key is stored encrypted and never shown again
  • RetroAchievements username and web API key (both optional) — used to read your achievements; the key is stored encrypted and never shown again. Your Steam, Xbox and RetroAchievements stats are read on demand and refreshed every 15 minutes at most; the last copy is kept until you unlink the platform or delete your account, and may still be shown while the platform cannot be reached
  • Blizzard stats — the region of your Battle.net account, the list of your World of Warcraft characters and your main character, kept until you unlink Battle.net or delete your account. Your World of Warcraft, StarCraft II and Diablo III profiles are read on demand and refreshed every 30 minutes at most; the last copy is kept until you unlink Battle.net or delete your account
  • FACEIT nickname (optional) — the nickname you declare and the player id FACEIT gives for it, kept until you remove the nickname or delete your account. Your Counter-Strike 2 level, Elo and stats are read on demand and refreshed every 10 minutes at most, your last match every 5 minutes at most; the last copy is kept until you remove the nickname or delete your account, and may still be shown while FACEIT cannot be reached
  • Bungie.net (Destiny 2) — your Bungie.net account and Destiny 2 membership ids, your Bungie Name and the authorization Bungie gives when you link (its renewal token, stored encrypted, valid 90 days and renewed as it is used), kept until you unlink Bungie.net or delete your account. Your Destiny 2 profile is read on demand with that authorization: the profile is refreshed every 2 minutes at most, the triumph score every 30 minutes at most; the last copy is kept until you unlink Bungie.net or delete your account
  • Riot ID (optional) — the name and tag you declare, your League of Legends server and the account id Riot gives for that Riot ID, kept until you remove the Riot ID or delete your account. Your League of Legends ranks and level and your Teamfight Tactics rank are read on demand and refreshed every 10 minutes at most, your game in progress every minute at most; the last copy is kept until you remove the Riot ID or delete your account
  • Chess.com and Lichess usernames (both optional) — used to read your ratings, record and games in progress from those sites' public profiles; Chess.com's player id and Lichess's account id are kept next to them. They are read on demand and refreshed every 10 minutes at most; the last copy is kept until you remove the name or delete your account
  • Error reports — when a page hits an error: your account id, the trail of your last actions (clicks, pages, requests) and a recording of the page, without what you type in fields, to reproduce the problem

Data Transfers

Your data is sent only to the Twitch and Steam APIs (and, if you set them, to speedrun.com, RetroAchievements, Riot Games, FACEIT, Chess.com and Lichess) for the application to function, and error reports to Sentry (sentry.io), through Catapult: your browser never contacts Sentry directly. No other third-party sharing.

Retention Period

Your data is kept for the lifetime of your account and deleted when you delete it; error reports for 90 days at most.

Your Rights

You can delete your account from the Settings page. For any request, contact the administrator by email.
